Our Story

Born in Mutare,
Built with Passion

JPX Food started in 2018 as a small restaurant on Herbert Chitepo Street. Founded by Chef Justice Paradza, our goal was simple — serve the freshest, tastiest food in the city.

Today, we deliver hundreds of fresh meals weekly across Mutare, combining authentic local flavours with modern convenience.
